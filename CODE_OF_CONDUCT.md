# Code of Conduct

## Expectations

Treat participants respectfully. Welcome constructive feedback, focus criticism on ideas and code, and respect differences in experience and perspective.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information without permission are prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer using a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive incident details in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and warn, restrict, or ban participants depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior.

Maintainers handling reports should protect the reporter's privacy and disclose information only as necessary to address the incident.
